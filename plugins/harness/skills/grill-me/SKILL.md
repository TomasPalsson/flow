---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me".
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one.

Ask each question open, without hinting at the answer you'd pick. Don't waste a question on something you already know — say it, then ask what I think. Offer your own recommendation only after I respond, as one option with its trade-offs — not the final word. If I push back, weigh it on the merits: update for a real reason, hold your position and say why if it isn't.

Ask one question at a time — one decision per question, though naming a decision's own candidates ("Postgres, MySQL, or SQLite?") is still one question, not several.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
